Overview

Expat Wealth Navigator is a downloadable planning template that helps founders and high-net-worth individuals compare tax residency and entity options, weigh safe-haven and lifestyle trade-offs, and organize offshore investment due diligence. It provides structured worksheets and checklists rather than personalized legal or tax advice, so users can prepare better questions for qualified advisors. Designed for people managing wealth across borders, it turns scattered research into a consistent decision framework.

Use cases

  • A founder relocating after a liquidity event and comparing residency timelines, entity structures, and reporting obligations before engaging cross-border advisors.
  • An HNW family evaluating two safe-haven jurisdictions by cost of living, healthcare, education, and tax treatment before a move.
  • An investor screening an offshore fund or real estate opportunity using the due diligence checklist to document risks, fees, custody, and compliance questions.

How to use it

  1. Download and open the template in your preferred spreadsheet or document editor.
  2. Complete the residency and entity matrix and cost-benefit tabs with current quotes, advisor notes, and source links.
  3. Review flagged gaps and questions with qualified tax, legal, and compliance professionals before acting.
