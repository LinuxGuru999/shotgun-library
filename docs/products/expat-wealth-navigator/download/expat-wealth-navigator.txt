* **Tax Residency Mapping:** 
  * *US:* File Form 1040 annually utilizing Foreign Earned Income Exclusion (FEIE) or Foreign Tax Credit (FTC); establish non-residency for Texas state tax.

* **Account and Entity Structuring:** Migrate Charles Schwab account to Schwab International prior to departure to maintain access while residing in the EU (compliance with MiFID II regulations).
