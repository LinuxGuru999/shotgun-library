Overview

Terminal Cheat Sheet Pack is a downloadable set of 20 printable one-page reference cards covering common shell and Git commands, organized for quick scanning during daily development. It helps developers who want a concise offline reminder of syntax, flags, and recovery steps without digging through man pages or browser tabs. The cards are designed as a reusable template, so teams can annotate, reorder, or adapt them to their own workflows.

Use cases

  • You are onboarding a junior developer and need a quick desk reference for everyday Git branching and commit commands.
  • You are debugging a detached HEAD or failed rebase and want a recovery recipe without searching docs under pressure.
  • You are preparing a workshop or internal wiki and want printable command examples to hand out or embed as templates.

How to use it

  1. Download the pack and extract the files to a local folder.
  2. Print the cards you need or open them in your browser/PDF viewer for quick reference.
  3. Keep the recovery cards nearby and adapt any template sections to your team's conventions.
