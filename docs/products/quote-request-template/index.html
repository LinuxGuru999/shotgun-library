Overview

The Quote Request Template is a standardized digital document designed for small business owners who are tired of vague, uncomparable estimates from suppliers. By laying out your exact project needs in a fill-in-the-blank format, it forces vendors to give you apples-to-apples pricing on the first try.

Use cases

  • Sourcing manufacturing or raw materials from multiple overseas or domestic vendors without missing hidden fees.
  • Hiring a marketing agency or web developer and needing strict adherence to a specific scope of work.
  • Requesting maintenance or facility repair bids where contractors usually try to upsell unnecessary services.

How to use it

  1. Download the template instantly as an editable Word or Google Doc.
  2. Fill in your specific project requirements using the built-in scope checklist.
  3. Email the completed document directly to your supplier list and paste their answers into the comparison table.
