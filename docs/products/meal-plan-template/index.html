Overview

This is a reusable digital meal plan template with a weekly grid, shopping list, and leftover tracker. It helps individuals who want flexible planning without rigid diets, especially when schedules change or ingredients are already on hand. You can adapt meals, reuse leftovers, and keep grocery trips focused.

Use cases

  • You planned dinners for five nights but three changed; move meals across days without rewriting the whole plan.
  • You have cooked rice and roasted vegetables left; log them to build a next-day lunch instead of tossing them.
  • You shop once weekly; generate a list from planned meals, then remove pantry items already owned.

How to use it

  1. Download the template and open it in your preferred compatible app.
  2. Fill the weekly grid with meals, then mark leftovers and build your shopping list.
  3. Save a copy each week and adjust swaps or portions as plans change.
