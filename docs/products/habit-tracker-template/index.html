Overview

This Habit Tracker Template is a downloadable planner for tracking one personal habit across a 30-day grid. It helps individuals build consistency by recording daily completion, watching a streak counter, and reflecting during a weekly review. It is a manual template, so progress depends on your own check-ins rather than automatic syncing or reminders.

Use cases

  • Someone starting a daily walking habit who wants a visible 30-day commitment grid
  • A person rebuilding a meditation routine after missed days and using streaks to stay motivated
  • An individual reviewing weekly patterns to decide whether a habit needs a smaller or more realistic target

How to use it

  1. Download the template and open it in your preferred spreadsheet or document app
  2. Enter your habit name and mark each day in the 30-day grid as you complete it
  3. Check the streak counter and fill out the weekly review to adjust your plan
