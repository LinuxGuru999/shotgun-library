Overview

Renewal Tracker Template is a personal tracking template for recording subscription names, renewal dates, prices, and cancellation notes. It helps you see upcoming renewals in a calendar view, review total monthly and annual costs, and follow a checklist before cancelling. It is a manual tracking tool, so reminders depend on you keeping it updated.

Use cases

  • You have several streaming, software, and app subscriptions and want one place to check renewal dates before they auto-charge.
  • You are reviewing your budget and need to compare monthly and annual subscription costs before deciding what to keep.
  • You want to cancel a service but need to confirm login, billing date, and cancellation steps so you do not get charged again.

How to use it

  1. Download the template file and open it in your spreadsheet or note app.
  2. Enter each subscription's name, cost, billing cycle, renewal date, and cancellation details.
  3. Check the calendar and cost summary regularly, and update or cancel items as dates change.
