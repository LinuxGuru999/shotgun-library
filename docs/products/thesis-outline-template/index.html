Overview

Thesis Outline Template is a downloadable planning document for students who need to structure a thesis into manageable sections. It includes a chapter map, milestone dates, and a citation tracker to keep research organized. It is a planning aid, not a substitute for advisor guidance or university formatting rules.

Use cases

  • A student starting a thesis who needs a clear chapter-by-chapter structure before drafting begins.
  • A student who has fallen behind schedule and needs milestone dates to plan catch-up work.
  • A student managing sources across multiple chapters who wants to avoid duplicate or missing citations.

How to use it

  1. Download the template and open it in your preferred document or spreadsheet editor.
  2. Fill in the chapter map and set milestone dates based on your deadline and advisor feedback.
  3. Update the citation tracker as you add, remove, or reorganize sources during research.
