Overview

Weekly Ops Dashboard is a one-page template for small business owners and operations leads to review key metrics, current priorities, and active risks in one weekly snapshot. It gives a consistent place to record what changed, what needs attention, and who owns each item, without replacing your accounting, CRM, or project tools. It works best as a lightweight operating rhythm you update manually each week.

Use cases

  • A solo founder prepares for a Monday planning session by carrying over last week's unfinished priorities and noting one new revenue or delivery risk.
  • A small retail owner reviews weekly sales, inventory, and staffing metrics alongside three store priorities before a staff meeting.
  • A service business manager tracks overdue invoices, client delivery risks, and top tasks for the week in one page shared with the owner.

How to use it

  1. Download the template file and save your own working copy.
  2. Open it in the compatible spreadsheet, document, or notes app listed with the download.
  3. Fill in this week's metrics, priorities, and risks, then review and update it before your next weekly meeting.
