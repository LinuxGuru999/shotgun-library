Overview

Subscription Killer Kit is a one-time digital bundle of checklists, guides, and tracking templates designed to help individuals find, evaluate, and cancel recurring software subscriptions. It is aimed at people who want to reduce monthly app and SaaS spending without subscribing to another service. The materials are practical and self-guided, so results depend on your own follow-through and the policies of each provider.

Use cases

  • You have forgotten active subscriptions across app stores, email receipts, and bank statements and need a structured way to find them.
  • You are considering replacing paid tools such as note-taking, password, or media apps with self-hosted or free alternatives.
  • You want a simple tracker to record cancellation dates, renewal deadlines, refund requests, and confirmation numbers.
  • You want to compare the effort of self-hosting against keeping a paid subscription before making a change.

How to use it

  1. Download the bundle and open the audit checklist to inventory recurring charges from your accounts and statements.
  2. Use the self-hosted swap guides to compare each subscription with lower-cost or self-hosted alternatives.
  3. Enter cancellation details into the tracker and follow each provider's official cancellation process, saving confirmations.
