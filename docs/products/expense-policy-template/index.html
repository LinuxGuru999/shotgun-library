Overview

This Expense Policy Template is a single-page, editable document that helps small businesses set clear spending categories, approval thresholds, and reimbursement steps for their teams. It is designed to reduce ad hoc expense questions and create a consistent process without adding complex finance software. Because it is a template, you adapt the limits, categories, and approvers to your company’s actual budget and local requirements.

Use cases

  • A five-person agency needs simple rules for client meals, software, and travel spending before its first business trip.
  • A founder hiring contractors wants written approval steps so receipts are submitted consistently each month.
  • A small retail team needs clear category limits for supplies and mileage reimbursements during busy seasons.

How to use it

  1. Download the file and open it in your preferred document editor.
  2. Replace the placeholder categories, limits, approvers, and timelines with your business details.
  3. Share the final version with your team and follow the stated receipt and reimbursement process.
